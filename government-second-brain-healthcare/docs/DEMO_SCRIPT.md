1. **Context (0:00–0:25):** The platform is an institutional memory that turns evidence, policy and independent role assessments into reviewable action proposals. All metrics and events are synthetic.
2. **T1 (0:25–1:05):** Select Stage 1. Completion falls from 76% to 53%, a 23-percentage-point decline. The service analyst requests funnel investigation; neither the model nor the executive receives an invented root cause or automatic approval.
3. **T2 (1:05–1:45):** Select Stage 2 and run again. A complaint export is reported to contain restricted data. The privacy and security agents block external AI transfer, quarantine ingestion, cite relevant UAE health-ICT sources, and escalate to authorized humans.
4. **T3 (1:45–2:25):** Select Stage 3. A purported outdated procedure proposes automatic eligibility rejection. The legal and clinical roles identify missing authority and block an automated decision. The institutional coordinator identifies needed human reviews.
5. **Evidence (2:25–3:00):** Open the Knowledge Base tab to demonstrate source URLs and vector search; open Audit and Evaluation to download a hash-linked audit chain. Clarify that role narratives are deterministic safety rules by default, with optional AI narrative generated solely from structured synthetic assessments.

## Possible Q&A

**Is this real patient data?** No. Neither clinical records nor actual government service telemetry are in this repository.

**Is it actually agentic?** It orchestrates independently scoped role-specific agents and a policy/oversight gate with progressive evidence. The offline mode uses explainable deterministic role assessment instead of an LLM. Optional narrative synthesis can be activated by explicit opt-in, but a deployable autonomous policy decision-maker would require additional implementation and validation.

**Why not automate eligibility?** Because medical and government eligibility determinations require verified policy authority, institutional responsibility, fairness checks and human oversight.

**Where is the open knowledge base?** `data/policies.json` contains curated, publicly referenceable metadata and summaries of official documents. It is a limited initial registry, not the full legislation corpus requested for a production system.
